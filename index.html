Teaching prompts

1. Keep M, k, mₑ, e and ζ fixed. Increase ω from below to above ωₙ. Why does the forcing amplitude grow with ω² while the displacement does not increase indefinitely?
2. At r ≈ 1, compare ζ = 0.02 and ζ = 0.20. What changes?
3. Double the eccentricity e. Predict the change in force and steady-state displacement.
4. Set ζ = 0 and r = 1. Explain why the ideal response amplitude grows with time instead of reaching steady state.
